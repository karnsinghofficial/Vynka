Questions, answered

How natural does Vynka actually sound?

Vynka responds in under 300ms and handles interruptions naturally. Most callers can't tell it's AI unless told

Which languages does it support?

40+ languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada and Marathi, with natural code-switching mid-call

Do I need a developer to set this up?

No. The no-code builder covers most flows, drop into the API for finer control

Is my data secure?

SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA-readiness, GDPR and India's DPDP Act compliance are all in progress, with regional data residency options in India, the US or the EU

Can a call be handed to a real person?

Yes. Vynka can transfer mid-call with full context, so nothing gets re-asked

Does Vynka handle outbound calls too?

Yes. Outbound campaigns for reminders and outreach, alongside every inbound call

How does billing work?

₹10 per connected call for your first 5,000 calls each month, then ₹9 per call up to 10,000. Above that, pricing is custom. Only connected calls are billed, unanswered attempts cost nothing
