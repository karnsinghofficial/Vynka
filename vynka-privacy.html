Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Template notice. This is a standard starting-point policy for an AI voice-calling business. It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. Have one review and localize it, especially the sections on call recording consent, GDPR and India's DPDP Act, before you rely on it or publish it live.

This policy explains what personal data Vynka ("Vynka," "we," "us") collects through our website and voice AI services, how we use it, and the choices available to you. It applies to visitors of this site, to the businesses that use Vynka (our "customers"), and to the individuals those customers' agents call or are called by.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us directly: name, phone number, email address, and preferred demo time, submitted through our forms.
  • Call data: when a Vynka agent places or answers a call for a customer, we process the call audio, an automated transcript, and metadata such as duration, timestamp, and outcome.
  • Account and integration data: information a customer syncs from connected tools, such as a CRM or calendar, when they enable an integration.
  • Usage data: pages visited, browser and device information, and similar data collected automatically, including through cookies.

2. Call recording and consent

Calls handled by a Vynka agent may be recorded and transcribed for service delivery, quality review, and analytics. Vynka's business customers are responsible for giving callers any notice, and obtaining any consent, required under the law of the jurisdictions where their calls take place. Vynka provides settings to support this, including configurable disclosure messages and data residency options.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Vynka service
  • To respond to inquiries and schedule demos
  • To send account, service, and security-related communications
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or technical issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing of information

We share information with service providers who help us run Vynka, such as hosting, telephony, and analytics providers, and with the third-party tools a customer chooses to connect. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights and users. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data retention

We keep personal data for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Call recordings and transcripts are retained according to each customer's configured retention settings, and are deleted or anonymized after that period unless a longer period is legally required.

6. Security and compliance

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA-readiness, GDPR, and India's DPDP Act compliance work is currently in progress; this section will be updated as certifications are completed.

7. International data transfers

Depending on the data residency option a customer selects, data may be processed in India, the United States, or the European Union. Where data crosses borders, we use appropriate contractual and technical safeguards.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the GDPR for individuals in the EU and the DPDP Act for individuals in India, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site and understand how it's used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site functionality.

10. Children's privacy

Vynka is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "last updated" date above.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@vynka.example.
